## Question and boundaries

F0 asks whether a fixed documentary set establishes independent experimentally supported BGC-to-product labels, metagenomic evaluation provenance, family/source-held-out evaluation feasibility and rights for the original two-component question. A label copied from antiSMASH predictions is circular for testing a model against antiSMASH. Chemical class or putative core structure is not the final full chemical structure. Identifying an unannotated cluster is not proof of a novel natural product, experimentally active function or biological discovery. No such narrower substitution will be promoted silently. Novel means no known-family/known-cluster match under a threshold fixed in F1. Unannotated or absent from MIBiG is not novelty: MIBiG documents characterised clusters, so absence is expected for most clusters. F0 sets no similarity threshold and performs no BLAST, KnownClusterBlast or family clustering; family definition and holdout rule are F1 items.

Potential future useful direction: distinguish genuine metagenomic/family transfer from similarity to known biosynthetic families and known-product retrieval. F0 is only documentary readiness. It cannot satisfy the user's success/discovery/judge chain. Full GO impossible at F0 because execution readiness is untested; at most GO-ready for separately registered F1.
